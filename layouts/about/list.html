ReachOut2Me is an open profile directory for creators, builders, and curious minds. Share your social accounts, your portfolio, your passion projects — whatever tells your story.

Find someone by their name, their work, their interests, or the place they call home. Follow a link. Start a conversation. See where it takes you.

Open by design.

Every profile is a small YAML file, reviewed through a pull request. Hugo turns those files into a fast static site. No database, no tracking, no algorithm deciding who matters.

The included profiles are examples. Replace them with real community members as you grow.
